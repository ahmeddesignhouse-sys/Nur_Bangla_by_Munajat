# Nur Bangla (`com.noorbangla.islam`) — v1.9 (versionCode 10)

Islamic Prayer Times, Quran, Qibla, Tasbih, Hijri Calendar, Dua, 99 Names, Ramadan, Zakat, Hajj & Umrah, Mosque & Halal Finder, and Daily Hadith & Ayah Share Studio for Android.

## v1.9 Highlights
- **1,095 Verified Hadiths & 1,095 Verified Quran Ayahs** bundled offline (`hadiths_catalog.json`, `ayahs_catalog.json`) rotating every 3 hours in `Asia/Dhaka` (`05:00–08:00`, `08:00–11:00`, `11:00–05:00` next day) with zero repeats across a full 365-day year, plus Slot 1 special-day badges (Friday/Jummah, Ramadan, Laylatul Qadr, Eid ul-Fitr, Eid ul-Adha, Arafah, Shab-e-Barat, Muharram/Ashura).
- **12 Share Card Themes & 5 Font Sizes** (`Small`, `Medium`, `Large`, `Extra Large`, `XXL`) with zero-clipping auto-fit across Story 9:16 and Post 1:1.
- **MP4 Story / Reels Video Share** (15s / 30s / 60s, 1080×1920 H.264 with Ken Burns zoom & looped background audio) + in-app live preview.
- **Honest Facebook Account Switch Helper** + Save Image & Copy Caption + tap/long-press App Download Link (no QR code).
- **Google Play Store Compliant Release**: in-app APK self-updater and `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` permission completely removed.
